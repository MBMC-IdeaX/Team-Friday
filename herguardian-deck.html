Fair Look at Today's Apps

Apps like bSafe already offer journey monitoring and preventive features alongside SOS. We build on that idea rather than replace it.

Nepal-Focused Route Comparison

General maps optimise for speed. Commuters in Nepal have little way to compare the safety trade-offs between route options.

Fragile Connections

Emergency flows that need a live connection can break when the network drops, so we queue SOS locally.

Our Solution

Compare, Prevent, Respond

Route comparison plus guardian support, built to help users compare relative risk before and during a journey.

Compare

A deterministic weighted score: crime risk (40%), community reports (25%), time of day (15%), lighting (10%), crowd activity (10%). Demo factors are synthetic.

Prevent

We show the shortest route and a "safest" route. Safest means the highest-scoring among the route alternatives OSRM returns, not every possible road.

Respond

One-tap SOS queues locally in an IndexedDB outbox if the network drops, with guardian tracking and audio recording. Video recording is not implemented yet.

Improve

Anonymous community reports feed back into the risk map, closing a community feedback loop.
