Prevent

Safest ≠ shortest

Shortest route

12 min · 4.1 km

score 34 — an unlit stretch with three prior reports

Safest route

15 min · 4.6 km

score 74 — lit, busy, and nobody has reported it

Three extra minutes. We sample ~60 points along each route's geometry and average the cell safety across them.

This is the screenshot judges remember. Most incumbents don't do it, or do it statically.
6
Respond

The panic button that can't not fire

A normal implementation POSTs to a server first. Go through one tunnel and it silently does nothing. Ours writes to the phone before it touches a network.

1 · write locallyIndexedDB first. "Fired" = persisted, not acknowledged
2 · call & texttel: / share sheet — OS-level, work with no signal
3 · stay loudWake lock keeps the screen on; alarm needs no network
4 · record10-second chunks, so a suspension keeps the evidence
5 · alertPush reaches a guardian whose browser is closed
Recording now · and a second tap reuses the live session instead of re-alerting everyone — nothing is ever blocked, because a rate limit that suppressed a second tap could kill the one call that matters.

Improve

The score is not a survey. It moves.

One anonymous report bumps the three nearest cells and re-anchors their decay. Nobody logs in. Nothing is traced.

Report → nearby scores change → the route other women are given changes. That's the moment the pitch turns.

9
Trust

The hard part was not the map

We don't verify identity — on purpose.

Linking a verified Aadhaar to SOS timestamps and GPS coordinates builds a searchable record of when and where a woman was in danger. That is a surveillance tool aimed at the victim.

Reports have no public read policy at all. The guardian session uuid is the credential. Optional magic-link sign-in exists — and never gates SOS.
Volunteering this reads as competence. Being caught on it doesn't.
10
Honesty

What this does not do

Crime data is seededPublic data is NCRB-level, not street-level. The pipeline and weights are real; the history is labelled // FAKE.
No offline routingOSRM is a network call. Offline still shows which direction is safer.
Nothing from a locked screenNative-only. tel: is the floor — which is why the call button is a real link, not an API call.
Voice needs signalSpeech recognition is cloud-based, so it's the one trigger that dies in a tunnel. Built last, for that reason.
Untested on hardwareOffline, two-phone tracking and push are code-verified only. We'll say so on stage.

Build

MapLibreNo API key. Risk overlay is drawn client-side from cached cells

The service worker, offline queue, alarm and charts are hand-rolled on platform APIs. No ML, no native build, no background jobs.
